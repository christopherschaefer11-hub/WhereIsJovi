# 📋 SLP Instructor Preparation Guide: "Where is Jovi?" Preposition & Following Directions Scene

**Designed for:** Shaila Schaefer's Elementary SLP Caseload (K–5th Grade, 1:1 or Small Groups)  
**Target Clinical Areas:** Spatial Prepositions (`ON`, `UNDER`, `IN`, `NEXT TO`, `BEHIND`, `IN FRONT OF`, `BETWEEN`, `ABOVE`), Following 1–3 Step Directions (`First/Then`, `Before/After`), Answering *Where* Questions, Expanding Utterances, and Pragmatic Barrier Games.

---

## 1. Quick 2-Minute Pre-Session Setup

1. **Choose One of 4 Real-World Settings (Top-Left Bar)**
   - **🌳 Field Club Yard:** Deck, fence, blue watering tray, patio chair, oak tree, and garden bush.
   - **🛋️ Living Room:** Patrol window, blue sofa, coffee table, laundry sock basket, and Jovi's dog bed.
   - **🍳 Chef Kitchen:** Refrigerator, counter, big red chef pot, slippery blue dish towel, food mat, and kitchen table.
   - **🏫 Speech Room:** Classroom door, whiteboard easel, story book bin, speech table, and cozy beanbag.
2. **Choose Your Clinical Activity Mode (Top-Right Bar)**
   - **🎯 Follow Directions:** Auto-generates and verifies 1-step, 2-step (*First/Then*), or complex temporal (*Before/After*) directions.
   - **🕵️ Hide & Seek Piggy:** Jovi hides Piggy invisibly in one of the 9 spatial spots! Students must ask a complete prepositional question (*"Is Piggy UNDER the table?"*) before tapping the spot to peek.
   - **🎨 Free Barrier Scene:** Open-ended placement where multiple characters (`Jovi`, `Piggy`, `Snoop`, `Squeaky Ball`, `Socks`, `Dinner Bowl`, `Bone`, `Squirrel`) can be arranged freely for barrier games or scene description.
3. **Zero-Frustration Tap-to-Place Controls**
   - Students tap a character/object in the **Step 1 Tray** and then tap any of the **9 labeled preposition spots** in the scene.

---

## 2. Recommended 25–30 Minute Therapy Session Flow

1. **Minutes 0–4 • Visual Contrast Warm-Up (`🃏 Preposition Deck`)**
   - Open the **🃏 Preposition Deck** tab and review 2 contrasting spatial cards (e.g., `ON` vs. `UNDER` or `IN FRONT OF` vs. `BEHIND`). Click **🔊 Hear Card** to model the sentence frame.
2. **Minutes 4–14 • Receptive Following Directions (`🎯 Follow Directions`)**
   - Select **1-Step**, **2-Step (First/Then)**, or **3-Step / Before & After**.
   - Have the student listen to the prompt (or click **🗣️ Speak Prompt**), repeat the plan, and place the items.
   - Log each turn in the purple **Shaila's Clinical Companion** drawer (`✅ Indep.`, `💡 Cued`, `🤝 Modeled`).
3. **Minutes 14–22 • Expressive Mystery Game (`🕵️ Hide & Seek Piggy`)**
   - Switch to **🕵️ Hide & Seek Piggy**. Require the student to say or build a prepositional phrase on the bottom **Spatial AAC Strip** (`PUT` / `WHERE` + `PIGGY` + `UNDER`) before clicking a spot to check.
4. **Minutes 22–28 • Role-Reversal Barrier Game & Documentation (`🎨 Free Barrier Scene` & `📊 SLP Session Data`)**
   - Let the student be the "Boss of Jovi" and give YOU (or a peer) a 2-step direction to place Jovi and Snoop in the scene.
   - Open **📊 SLP Session Data** and click **📋 Copy Progress Note** for an instant SOAP summary.
