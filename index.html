📋 SLP Instructor Preparation Guide: “Where is Jovi?”

How to use Jovi, Piggy, and Snoop to target receptive/expressive prepositions, 1–3 step directions, WH-questions, and peer barrier games.

1 • Frictionless Placement

Tap-to-Place on Any Screen

Students simply tap a character in the top tray (🐕‍🦺 Jovi, 🐷 Piggy, 🐕 Snoop, or a prop) and then tap any of the 8 labeled preposition zones in the scene (ON, UNDER, IN, NEXT TO, BEHIND, IN FRONT OF, BETWEEN, ABOVE). Multiple characters can share the scene at once!

2 • Three Clinical Modes

Receptive, Expressive & Pragmatic

  • 🎯 Follow Directions: Auto-checks 1-step, 2-step (First/Then), and complex (Before/After) directions.
  • 🕵️ Hide & Seek Piggy: Hides Piggy secretly so students must ask prepositional questions (“Is Piggy UNDER the deck?”) before peeking!
  • 🎨 Free Barrier Scene: Students give each other multi-step directions to build matching scenes.
3 • Spatial AAC Strip

Multimodal Preposition Modeling

The bottom AAC strip features color-coded spatial prepositions (ON, IN, UNDER, NEXT TO, BEHIND, IN FRONT, BETWEEN) alongside core verbs (PUT, LOOK, WHERE) so students can build and speak full sentences.

⏱️ Recommended 25–30 Minute Therapy Session Flow

Minutes 0–4 • Contrast Warm-Up

1. Preposition Deck Preview

Open 🃏 Preposition Deck to review the target spatial pair (e.g., ON vs. UNDER or IN FRONT OF vs. BEHIND) with Jovi and Piggy visual anchor cards.

Minutes 4–14 • Receptive Drills

2. Following Directions Mode

In 🗺️ Interactive Scene, select Tier 1 (1-Step), Tier 2 (First/Then), or Tier 3 (Before/After). Have the student listen (or click 🗣️ Speak Prompt) and place the items.

Minutes 14–22 • Expressive Turn

3. Hide & Seek Piggy

Switch to 🕵️ Hide & Seek Piggy. Require the student to formulate a full prepositional guess (verbal or via the AAC strip) before tapping a hotspot to check if Piggy is hiding there!

Minutes 22–28 • Peer Barrier Game

4. Student becomes the Director

Switch to 🎨 Free Barrier Scene. Let the student give YOU (or a peer) a 2-step direction to place Jovi, Snoop, and the dinner bowl, then export your SOAP note in 📊 SLP Session Data!
